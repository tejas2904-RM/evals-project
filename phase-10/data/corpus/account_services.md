# Account services

## KYC
Before investing, every investor must complete KYC (Know Your Customer) once through any KRA-registered
intermediary. You will need your PAN, an address proof and a photograph. Aadhaar-based e-KYC is accepted.

## How to redeem units
Log in to the Asterion investor portal or mobile app, choose the scheme and folio, enter the amount or
number of units and confirm. Redemption proceeds are credited to your registered bank account, usually
within 3 working days for equity schemes and 1 working day for liquid and debt schemes.

## Cut-off times
- Equity and debt schemes: purchase requests received with funds by 3:00 PM get the same day's NAV.
- Liquid fund: purchase requests received with funds by 1:30 PM get the previous calendar day's NAV.

## Account statements
A consolidated account statement (CAS) is emailed monthly when there is a transaction, and every six
months otherwise.
