# Asterion Horizon Short Term Debt Fund

**Category:** Short Duration Fund (open-ended debt scheme)
**Benchmark:** CRISIL Short Duration Debt Index
**Fund manager:** Kavita Iyer (since March 2023)
**Riskometer:** Low to Moderate

## Investment objective
To generate regular income by investing in debt and money market instruments with a portfolio
Macaulay duration of 1 to 3 years.

## Fees and charges
- Total expense ratio (TER), Direct Plan: 0.35% per annum
- Total expense ratio (TER), Regular Plan: 0.95% per annum
- Exit load: 0.25% if units are redeemed within 30 days of allotment; nil after 30 days.

## Investment minimums
- Minimum SIP: Rs 500 per month
- Minimum lump sum: Rs 1,000

## Risk disclosure
Debt funds carry interest-rate risk and credit risk. Returns are not guaranteed.
