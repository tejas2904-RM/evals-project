# Asterion Tax Saver ELSS Fund

**Category:** ELSS (open-ended equity-linked savings scheme with a statutory lock-in of 3 years and tax benefit)
**Benchmark:** NIFTY 500 TRI
**Fund manager:** Priya Raman (since August 2019)
**Riskometer:** Very High

## Lock-in period
Every investment, including each SIP instalment, is locked in for 3 years from the date of allotment.
Units cannot be redeemed or switched during the lock-in.

## Fees and charges
- Total expense ratio (TER), Direct Plan: 0.55% per annum
- Total expense ratio (TER), Regular Plan: 1.85% per annum
- Exit load: Nil (units are subject to the 3-year lock-in).

## Investment minimums
- Minimum SIP: Rs 500 per month
- Minimum lump sum: Rs 500

## Risk disclosure
Mutual fund investments are subject to market risks. Returns are not guaranteed. Tax benefits depend on
the investor's tax regime; please consult a tax advisor.
