# Asterion Liquid Fund

**Category:** Liquid Fund (open-ended debt scheme investing in instruments maturing within 91 days)
**Benchmark:** CRISIL Liquid Debt Index
**Fund manager:** Sanjay Kulkarni (since February 2021)
**Riskometer:** Low to Moderate

## Investment objective
To provide liquidity and reasonable returns by investing in money market and debt instruments with
maturity of up to 91 days.

## Fees and charges
- Total expense ratio (TER), Direct Plan: 0.12% per annum
- Total expense ratio (TER), Regular Plan: 0.22% per annum
- Exit load (graded, as per SEBI rules for liquid funds): 0.007% if redeemed on day 1, decreasing each
  day, and nil from day 7 onwards.

## Investment minimums
- Minimum SIP: Rs 500 per month
- Minimum lump sum: Rs 500

## Risk disclosure
Liquid funds carry low but non-zero credit and interest-rate risk. Returns are not guaranteed.
