# Asterion Bluechip Value Fund

**Category:** Value Fund (open-ended equity scheme following a value investment strategy)
**Benchmark:** NIFTY 500 TRI
**Fund manager:** Arjun Mehta (since January 2022)
**Riskometer:** Very High

## Investment objective
To generate long-term capital appreciation by investing in companies that trade below their intrinsic
value, with a bias towards large-cap stocks. There is no assurance that the objective will be achieved.

## Fees and charges
- Total expense ratio (TER), Direct Plan: 0.74% per annum
- Total expense ratio (TER), Regular Plan: 1.92% per annum
- Exit load: 1% if units are redeemed within 180 days of allotment; nil after 180 days.

## Investment minimums
- Minimum SIP: Rs 1,000 per month
- Minimum lump sum: Rs 5,000

## Risk disclosure
Mutual fund investments are subject to market risks. Returns are not guaranteed.
