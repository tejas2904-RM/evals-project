# Frequently asked questions

## What is NAV?
Net Asset Value (NAV) is the per-unit market value of a scheme's assets after expenses. Units are bought
and redeemed at the applicable NAV.

## What is an exit load?
An exit load is a fee charged as a percentage of NAV when you redeem units before a specified period.
Each scheme states its own exit load and period in its factsheet.

## What is the difference between Direct and Regular plans?
Both plans hold the same portfolio. The Regular plan includes distributor commission, so its total
expense ratio is higher than the Direct plan's.

## What is a SIP?
A Systematic Investment Plan lets you invest a fixed amount at regular intervals, usually monthly.

## Does Asterion give investment advice?
No. Asterion Mutual Fund provides scheme information only. For advice on which scheme suits you, please
consult a SEBI-registered investment advisor.
