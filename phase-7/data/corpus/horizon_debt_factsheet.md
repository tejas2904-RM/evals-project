# Asterion Horizon Debt Fund

**Category:** Medium to Long Duration Fund (open-ended debt scheme)
**Benchmark:** CRISIL Medium to Long Duration Debt Index
**Fund manager:** Kavita Iyer (since June 2020)
**Riskometer:** Moderate

## Investment objective
To generate income by investing in a portfolio of government securities and high-quality corporate
bonds with a portfolio Macaulay duration of 4 to 7 years.

## Fees and charges
- Total expense ratio (TER), Direct Plan: 0.28% per annum
- Total expense ratio (TER), Regular Plan: 0.81% per annum
- Exit load: Nil. There is no exit load on this scheme.

## Investment minimums
- Minimum SIP: Rs 500 per month
- Minimum lump sum: Rs 1,000

## Risk disclosure
Debt funds carry interest-rate risk and credit risk. Returns are not guaranteed.
