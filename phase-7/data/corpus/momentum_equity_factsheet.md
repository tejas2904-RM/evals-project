# Asterion Momentum Equity Fund

**Category:** Thematic Fund (open-ended equity scheme following a momentum theme)
**Benchmark:** NIFTY 200 Momentum 30 TRI
**Fund manager:** Rohan Desai (since November 2022)
**Riskometer:** Very High

## Investment objective
To generate long-term capital appreciation by investing in mid- and large-cap companies that show
strong recent price momentum, rebalanced every six months. There is no assurance that the objective
will be achieved.

## Fees and charges
- Total expense ratio (TER), Direct Plan: 0.89% per annum
- Total expense ratio (TER), Regular Plan: 2.05% per annum
- Exit load: 1% if units are redeemed within 90 days of allotment; nil after 90 days.

## Investment minimums
- Minimum SIP: Rs 1,000 per month
- Minimum lump sum: Rs 5,000

## Risk disclosure
Momentum strategies can underperform sharply when market leadership changes. Returns are not
guaranteed and the fund can lose value.
